# Rabovel — Colosseum research and pitch workspace

Prepared 7 October 2026 for Crypto World's Fair. This is a working strategy and evidence package, not completed application answers or a founder script.

- [Business plan](business-plan.md): customer, product, competition, operating model, market, economics and validation plan.
- [Research register](research-register.md): sources, dates, confidence and limits.
- [Pitch outline](pitch-outline.md): ten core slides, founder prompts, demo storyboard and interview preparation.
- [Editable PowerPoint](rabovel-pitch-blueprint.pptx): ten core slides and five appendices, with research links and notes.
- [Browser preview](rabovel-pitch-preview.html): the same slide content, printable and usable offline.
- [Financial scenarios](financial-scenarios.csv): explicitly hypothetical annual capacity scenarios.

The founder confirmed a BNB Chain internship, junior development work at Neo Financial, Nigerian finance experience elsewhere in the team, and a demo-only product. The selected first customer is a broker or issuing intermediary buying cNGN funding, distribution and settlement infrastructure. Customer demand, willingness to pay, asset rights and the operating permissions remain unvalidated.

Before recording, replace the founder prompts with your own words, add the actual team names and contributions, insert product captures from a verified run, and disclose development completed before the event. Retain the demo and assumption labels.
