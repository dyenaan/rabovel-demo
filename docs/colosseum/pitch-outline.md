# Pitch and demo blueprint

Use the [editable deck](rabovel-pitch-blueprint.pptx) as a structure for your own presentation. It contains evidence and founder prompts, not application answers or a finished narration. Core deck target: **2:45**; separate demo target: **2:40**, below the FAQ's three-minute limit. Appendices are for an interview, not the timed pitch. [Presentation requirements](https://colosseum.com/hackathon).

The slides deliberately avoid a broad TAM headline, fictitious user quotes and unverified partnerships. A narrower company story is more defensible with the current evidence. Keep research and hypotheses visible until you replace them with genuine observations.

## Founder edits before recording

1. Add team names and precise roles, including one relevant task from each person's experience. BNB Chain and Neo Financial are prior experience, not endorsements or partners.
2. Explain your motivation through an observed problem. If you have not observed it firsthand, label the customer hypothesis honestly.
3. Record one verified integrated devnet purchase and replace the product-capture prompt. Do not pass mocked screens off as execution.
4. Select the intended asset-rights model and explain what remains unapproved. Do not describe simulated inventory as legal shares.
5. Add any real interview or commitment only after it occurs, with its date and scope. Demo interactions are not user adoption.
6. Disclose the pre-event baseline and event-period development. Provide repository access; identify third-party components accurately.

| Limits and next product work | 20s | Separate eligibility preparation, simulated backing/KYC, production ledger/recovery and legal ownership still pending |
| **Total** | **160s** | |

Before filming, execute the flow and preserve its public transaction identifier and timestamps. Verify successful outcome rather than assuming a success toast proves settlement. A demo that fails intermittently needs correction or an accurately labeled recording of a verified run; do not invent an explorer link.

Optional live interview demonstration: insufficient funds or inventory rejection, then explain an ambiguous RPC outcome and how durable reconciliation will resolve it. Do not claim implemented recovery merely because the backend exposes a `reconciliation_required` status.

## Interview preparation — 15 minutes

Prepare short answers with evidence rather than another long deck:

- **Why blockchain?** Compare prefunding, complete costs and record quality against a bank API; explain exactly which transfers share a transaction.
- **What does a token legally represent?** Identify issuer/custodian, holder rights, register authority, exit and who resolves an ownership dispute.
- **Why will brokers buy?** Show a real costly workflow, budget owner, alternative vendor and quantified willingness to pay when available.
- **How does this relate to NGX/NASD/CSCS?** Explain intended integration and permissions without claiming a relationship that does not exist.
- **What happens when funding, allotment or a transaction fails?** Separate onchain atomicity from bank operations and demonstrate the ledger/recovery plan.
- **What is the commercial bottleneck?** Discuss procurement, liquidity, compliance and integrations, not only network throughput.
- **Is the market large enough?** Walk through the qualified-buyer and annual-volume model; discuss repeat use and gated expansion.
- **What traction is real?** State demo stage clearly and distinguish interviews, unpaid pilots, signed commitments and paid use.
- **Why this team?** Tie individual experience and contributions to the next customer and engineering milestones.
- **What did you build during the event?** Explain the start-date baseline and show the actual changes, third-party composition and current demo.
